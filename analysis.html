Analysis

This page will describe the process of the experiment and how it wentTo calculate the friction coefficient of the pencil block, the experiment is set up with a mass of 200g placed onto the pencil block, while a mass of 100g (150g for the canvas) is attached to the end of the string.

The two farthest pencils start 22.86cm from the end of the pulley clamp, and travel a distance of 18.415cm. The front pencil (closest to the pulley) starts at 4.445cm, but still travels the same distance.

Coefficient is determined based on the average from all the pencils, not the pencils individually.
